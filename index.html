Data, definitions and limits
  • Data: Telco Customer Churn (IBM sample, Kaggle), team-cleaned file, one row per customer (7,043 customers). Churned = the customer left.
  • Churn rate = customers who left ÷ customers in the group. Revenue lost = monthly bills of customers who left.
  • Segments combine contract type (no contract = month-to-month) and internet service. Add-on services are compared among internet customers only.
  • One snapshot: it shows associations, not causes. Contract, tenure and fibre overlap, so any retention offer should be tested against a control group.
  • Confidence intervals: 95% Wilson intervals on each churn rate. Figures in the dashboard use one decimal; slides round to whole numbers.
  • One snapshot of a fictional company: no trend over time and no industry benchmark are available.
  • "Otherwise alike": average marginal effects from a logistic regression on all customers (contract, internet type, online security, tech support, payment method, paperless billing, senior, dependents, tenure). The model and its code are in Sources.
